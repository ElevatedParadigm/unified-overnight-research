## 🔥 Fire Force — Elemental Force Matrix

The **Fire Force** represents rapid discharge, sudden strikes, and transformation through burning. It correlates strongly with the Universal Bridge [[124]] and Trinity [[777]].

- 🔗 Chain-reaction propagation
