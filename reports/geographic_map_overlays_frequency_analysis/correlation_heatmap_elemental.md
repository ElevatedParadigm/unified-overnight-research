This document contains complete ASCII heatmaps visualizing elemental correlations across cultural, military, geographic, and eschatological domains using the heat scale encoding: `░ ▒ ▓ █ . o O ^`

```

    ▓ = Medium-High correlation (0.60-0.84)
    ▒ = Medium correlation (0.35-0.59)
    ░ = Low correlation (<0.35 but >0)
    . = Minimal/trace correlation
    o = Negligible correlation
    O = Null/absent correlation
```
