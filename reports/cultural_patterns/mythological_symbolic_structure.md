# Mythological & Symbolic Structure Analysis

## Overview
This document examines mythological structures, symbolic systems, and archetypal patterns that may encode universal principles.

## Major Mythological Systems

### 1. **Greek Mythology**
- **Cosmology:** Cosmic egg, Uranus-Gea origin
- **Pantheon Hierarchy:** Olympian order structure
- **Heroic Cycle:** Twelve labors of Heracles pattern
- **Afterlife:** Hades realm transitions

### 2. **Norse Cosmology**
- **Nine Worlds Structure:** Yggdrasil tree connections
- **Ragnarök Cycle:** Inevitable destruction/rebirth
- **God Deaths:** Odin, Thor, Loki prophecies

### 3. **Egyptian Afterlife**
- **Weighing of Heart Ritual:** Ma'at balance test
- **Duat Journey:** Underworld navigation steps
- **Osiris Resurrection:** Death and rebirth cycle

### 4. **Mesopotamian Creation**
- **Enuma Elish:** Tiamat/Abzu chaos waters
- **Kingdom of the Dead:** Irkalla underworld
- **Flood Myth:** Ziusudra/Utnapishtim survival

## Symbolic Structure Categories

### Pattern H1: Binary Oppositions

| Domain | Dual Aspect | Symbolic Meaning |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| Creation | Order/Chaos | Structured emergence from void |
| Death | Life/Death | Cyclical transformation |
| Knowledge | Hidden/Revealed | Initiation threshold |
| Gender | Male/Female | Complementary opposites |

### Pattern H2: Trinary Structures

Many myths organize around groups of three:
- **Greek:** Three Fates (Clotho, Lachesis, Atropos)
- **Buddhist:** Three bodies of Buddha
- **Egyptian:** Osiris, Isis, Horus triad
- **Christian:** Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit)

### Pattern H3: Cyclical Time Models
